The conventional wisdom used to be that building a massive product required a massive team. Today, small, highly focused teams are consistently outmaneuvering large corporations.

With AI assistants handling boilerplate code, automated testing, and initial design scaffolding, individual developers possess the output capacity of an entire department from a decade ago. 

Agility is the new currency. When you don't have to navigate three layers of management to approve a feature swap, you can ship exactly what your users need the moment they ask for it.
