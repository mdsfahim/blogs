Some of the best storytelling happening today isn't found in three-hour blockbusters or ten-episode streaming series. It is found in short films.

Constrained by budget and time, directors are forced to abandon filler. Every shot, line of dialogue, and sound cue must serve the core narrative immediately. 

This format is the purest distillation of a director's vision. It requires immense discipline to introduce characters, establish stakes, and deliver a satisfying emotional arc in under fifteen minutes.
