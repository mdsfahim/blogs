A cluttered desk leads to a cluttered mind. When building a workspace for deep work, remove everything that doesn't actively contribute to your daily tasks.

Start by clearing the surface completely. Add back only your computer, input devices, and a single notebook. Keep cables hidden and utilize soft, indirect lighting to reduce screen glare. 

When your environment is quiet, your focus naturally sharpens, making it easier to slip into a flow state and tackle complex problems without distraction.
