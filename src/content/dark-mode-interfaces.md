Designing for dark mode is not just about inverting colors. Pure white text on a pure black background creates severe eye strain and visual halation for many readers.

Instead, start with a dark gray canvas like `#121212`. Use slightly desaturated colors for your primary accents so they don't vibrate against the dark background. 

Most importantly, establish a clear semantic color system using CSS variables early in your project. This ensures your light and dark themes stay perfectly synchronized as your site grows.
